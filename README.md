## ![Glow Ink Plus mod](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/DfZhZ0LX/images/f560ac576417ab1674de4308781a4dbb38811ee3.png)

## **Features**

This mod adds new uses to the glow ink sac that was added in version 1.17. With this mod you can make wool, carpets,
beds, stained-glass, candles, glass panes, concrete, glazed terracotta, terracotta, concrete powder, leather armor,
banners, shields, and even sheep glow with emissive rendering (perfect for use with shaders).

*The wool textures of the texture packs are supported.*

---

### **Glow Wool**

Craft any wool with a Glow Ink Sac to create a glowing variant that emits a low light level and renders at full
brightness.

### **Glow Beds**

Craft any bed with a Glow Ink Sac, or craft glow wool with planks, to create a glowing bed variant. Glow beds work
exactly like vanilla beds (sleeping, setting spawn) with added emissive rendering.

![No shaders](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/DfZhZ0LX/images/9d5bc880b803305b689b6e4bfbc49af4885255f8.png)

### **Glow Stained Glass**

emissive rendering, compatible with beacons.

### **Glow Candles**

Craft any candle with a Glow Ink Sac to create a glowing candle. Place up to 4 per block and light them — the glow
effect stacks with the candle's natural light.

### **Glowing Sheep**

Right-click a sheep with a Glow Ink Sac to make its wool glow. The glowing state persists and is visible to all players
in multiplayer. Shearing a glowing sheep drops 1-3 Glow Wool items matching its dye color.

![No shaders](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/DfZhZ0LX/images/b00ccad3157bce144de1182914f82a2d61761c6d.png)
![With shaders](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/DfZhZ0LX/images/9544f1eff17ac500d45fee66ce71ca04166d13f7.png)

### **Glowing Leather Armor**

Place any leather armor piece in a Smithing Table with a Glow Ink Sac (no template needed) to make it glow at full
brightness. Dyed leather armor retains its color while glowing. The effect can be washed off in a water cauldron.

![No shaders](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/DfZhZ0LX/images/f14af6a97725004a392b047f32dd62c9062ff7e0.png)
![With shaders](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/DfZhZ0LX/images/22d9499ab4f03d99bd8f4353f8103f8dee9a8029.png)

### **Glowing Banners**

Combine any banner with a Glow Ink Sac in a Crafting Table to make it glow. All patterns, colors, and custom names are
preserved. Glowing banners render at full brightness both when placed in the world and when held in hand or displayed in
item frames.

![No shaders](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/DfZhZ0LX/images/c9ab403122d5d0efe705768338a1afb2efd34929.png)

---

### **Glowing Shields**

Combine a decorated shield (one with a banner pattern) with a Glow Ink Sac in a Crafting Table to make it glow.
Alternatively, applying a glowing banner to a shield will automatically transfer the glow effect. Glowing shields render
at full brightness when held.

![No shaders](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/DfZhZ0LX/images/86c0438764d7d32007710aca6cd4da867a45d20a.png)
![With shaders](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/DfZhZ0LX/images/f59ee5fdc0eb70c63ad194905c69257f7d39d976.png)

### **Additional Info**

- All glowing items display a yellow **"Glowing"** tooltip
- Glowing leather armor can be washed off in a water cauldron
- All 16 dye colors are supported for wool, carpets, beds, stained-glass, candles, glass panes, concrete, glazed
  terracotta, terracotta, concrete powder, banners, and sheep
- Compatible with Fabric, NeoForge, and Quilt
